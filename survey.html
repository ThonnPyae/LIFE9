ABOUT THIS SURVEY

Every Response Makes a Difference.

Disasters can affect anyone, anywhere. From floods and earthquakes to fires and extreme weather, being informed and prepared can make a meaningful difference.

This survey aims to understand people's experiences, knowledge and preparedness regarding different types of disasters in Myanmar. Your responses will help us identify awareness gaps and encourage safer communities.
