UNDERSTANDING THE RISK

Why Disaster Preparedness Matters

Myanmar faces natural hazards such as floods, cyclones, earthquakes and landslides. These events can damage homes, disrupt essential services and put lives at risk.

Although we cannot prevent every natural hazard, understanding the risks and knowing how to respond can help reduce harm. Access to reliable information is an important first step towards safer, more resilient communities.

SMALL STEPS, BIG DIFFERENCE

Be Prepared, Not Scared.

You cannot prevent every disaster, but you can take steps to reduce its impact on you and your community.

01

Prepare an Emergency Kit

Keep essential supplies ready, including drinking water, food, a flashlight, a first-aid kit, and important documents.

02

Make a Safety Plan

Know your evacuation routes, identify a safe meeting place, and discuss what to do with your family before an emergency.

Stay Informed
