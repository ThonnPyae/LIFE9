HIGH-RISK AREAS IN MYANMAR

Explore the natural disasters that threaten different regions of Myanmar, discover which communities are most vulnerable, and learn why some areas face greater risks.

High-Risk Regions & What They Face

Rakhine State

Exposed to cyclones, storm surges, and coastal flooding.

Coastal communities face threats from severe weather and powerful waves.

Yangon Region

Intensive urban flooding, cyclones, and accidental fires.

Overcrowded cities with clogged drainage systems trap seasonal monsoon rains and increase structural fire risks.

Ayeyarwady Region

Severe riverine flooding and coastal storm surges.

Low-lying delta terrain sits right at sea level where major rivers bottle up and overflow during heavy monsoon rains.

Bago Region

Chronic river flooding and high-impact earthquakes.

Multiple major river basins converge in this flat region while the active Sagaing fault line runs directly underneath.

Sagaing Region

Destructive earthquakes, river floods, and severe droughts.

The active Sagaing Fault splits the region open while local weather shifts create a flooded north and a parched south.

Mandalay & Magway Regions

Extreme droughts, severe heatwaves, and earthquakes.

Positioned in the rain shadow of the western mountains, these dry plains lack water while sitting near major tectonic fault lines.

Chin State

Destructive landslides and sudden flash floods. and strong earthquakes.

Steep mountain slopes and unstable terrain rapidly collapse under the weight of heavy, concentrated monsoon downpours.

Kachin State

Massive landslides and mudslides, High-impact earthquakes

Heavy seasonal rains combined with melting mountain snow completely overwhelm the upper Ayeyarwady river basins.
