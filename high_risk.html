REGIONAL OVERVIEW

Disasters in
Different Areas

Learn how geography influences the risks faced by communities across Myanmar.

WESTERN MYANMAR

Chin State

Main risks: Landslides, flash floods and earthquakes. Steep mountain slopes can become unstable during heavy monsoon rainfall.

WESTERN MYANMAR

Rakhine State

Main risks: Cyclones, storm surges and coastal flooding. Coastal communities are exposed to powerful winds and storm-related flooding.

SOUTHERN MYANMAR

Yangon Region

Main risks: Urban flooding, cyclones and extreme heat. Heavy rainfall and limited drainage capacity can cause flooding in urban neighbourhoods.

DELTA REGION

Ayeyarwady Region

Main risks: River floods, cyclones and storm surges. Low-lying delta communities are vulnerable to overflowing rivers and coastal hazards.

CENTRAL MYANMAR

Sagaing Region

Main risks: Earthquakes, floods and droughts. The Sagaing Fault creates significant earthquake hazards in this region.

NORTHERN MYANMAR

Kachin State

Main risks: Landslides, floods and earthquakes. Mountainous terrain and heavy seasonal rainfall increase landslide and flooding risks.

KNOW THE RISKS

Understanding Disaster Risks

Learn how different natural hazards can affect communities across Myanmar.

01 / FLOODING

Rising Waters

Heavy rainfall, overflowing rivers and poor drainage can cause flooding in vulnerable communities.

WATER-RELATED HAZARD
01 / FLOODING

Did You Know?

Flooding can damage homes, roads and farmland. Low-lying areas and communities near rivers are especially vulnerable.

STAY INFORMED AND PREPARED
02 / LANDSLIDES

Unstable Slopes

Intense rainfall can weaken steep slopes, increasing the possibility of landslides.

LAND-RELATED HAZARD
02 / LANDSLIDES

Did You Know?

Mountainous areas such as Chin and Kachin States can face greater landslide risks during heavy monsoon rainfall.

WATCH FOR WARNING SIGNS
03 / CYCLONES

Coastal Storms

Strong winds, heavy rain and storm surges can affect coastal communities.

WEATHER-RELATED HAZARD
03 / CYCLONES

Did You Know?

Coastal areas of Rakhine and the Ayeyarwady Delta are exposed to cyclones and storm surges.

FOLLOW WEATHER WARNINGS
04 / EXTREME HEAT

Rising Temperatures

Prolonged hot weather can increase health risks and contribute to water shortages.

CLIMATE-RELATED HAZARD
04 / EXTREME HEAT

Did You Know?

Extreme heat can cause heat-related illnesses, especially among elderly people, children and outdoor workers.

STAY COOL AND HYDRATED
