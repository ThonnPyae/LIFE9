All You Need to Know

LIFE9 raises awareness about disaster risks in Myanmar through safety tips, high-risk area information, past disaster data, and emergency preparedness guidance.

Myanmar experiences natural disasters such as floods, cyclones, earthquakes, landslides, droughts, and fires. These disasters can damage homes, disrupt communities, and threaten people's safety.

Prepare an emergency kit, keep important documents safe, learn evacuation routes, save emergency contact numbers, and follow official weather forecasts and warnings.

We can help elderly people, children, and people with disabilities by sharing safety information, helping them reach safe shelters, and checking on their well-being.
