About Us

Our Mission

To reduce disaster risks across Myanmar communities by providing accessible knowledge and practical preparation tools. We are dedicated to creating a digital infrastructure that equips individuals with essential resources to build robust emergency action plans, ensuring households are prepared before hazards strike to protect lives and minimize losses.


Our Vision

To build a disaster-resilient Myanmar where every family is safe, proactive, and well-informed. We aspire to cultivate a nationwide culture of readiness using technology and practical risk guidance, ensuring long-term safety, security, and preparedness for future generations.

Our Website Purpose

The purpose of our website AKA LIFE9 is to serve as a centralized, accessible digital hub that helps communities across Myanmar prepare for natural hazards before they strike.

Instead of focusing only on past disasters or statistics, the website provides practical, action-oriented resources such as interactive toolkits, emergency checklists, and localized hazard mapping.

Our Direction

Our Objectives & Ambitions

Proactive Safety

Encouraging early action and family preparedness well before official warnings are issued.

Nationalized Action

Tailored risk insights for Myanmar's regions (Sagaing Fault, Ayeyarwady Delta, Rakhine Coast).

Youth-Led Education

Presenting technical DRR knowledge in clear, engaging and accessible language for young learners.

Open Digital Access

Providing free, web-based tools accessible across low-bandwidth mobile devices.
